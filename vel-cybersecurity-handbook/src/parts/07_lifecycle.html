Part 7 — Client Engagement Lifecycle (Stages 01–15)

Every client engagement follows the same fifteen stages. A stage is finished only when its output exists and its gate is signed. No testing happens before Stage 02 is complete.

Never commit sensitive material

Passwords, tokens, customer data, raw scanner output with exploitable detail, and unsanitised evidence do not go into an ordinary Git repository. They live in the approved, restricted-access, encrypted evidence store. The repository holds only sanitised documents and references. Add secret scanning and a pre-commit hook to every project repository.

7.5 Final report outline

  1. Executive summary: overall posture, top risks, decisions needed; no exploit detail.
  2. Scope, authorisation reference and limitations (what was not tested).
  3. Methodology and tools (with versions) and standards used (OWASP WSTG, CIS, NIST).
  4. Assets assessed and risk summary chart.
  5. Findings by severity, using the record in 7.4.
  6. Business impact and risk ratings explained.
  7. Remediation plan with owners, dates and quick wins.
  8. Retest status and residual risk.
  9. Restricted technical annex (need-to-know), evidence index.
Honest limitations

State the testing window, access level, excluded systems and the fact that an assessment is a point-in-time sample. Never claim the client is "secure" or "compliant". Say what was found, what was fixed and what remains.

7.6 Managed security: what is and is not included

Typically included (when contracted)Not included unless explicitly agreed
  • Vulnerability tracking and monthly rescans (T1/T2 per RoE)
  • Patch verification and configuration checks
  • Alert triage within stated business hours
  • Quarterly access reviews
  • Incident coordination and evidence guidance
  • Monthly security report; periodic reassessment
  • 24/7 monitoring (only if staffed and tooled to deliver it)
  • Hands-on forensic investigation or legal advice
  • Regulatory notification decisions
  • Fixing client application code
  • Third-party licence costs and cloud platform costs
  • Certification or independent audit

Pricing, margins and recurring revenue (hours, licences, storage, retests, support) are the subject of Module 3. One-time assessment fees and monthly managed fees are always quoted separately.
