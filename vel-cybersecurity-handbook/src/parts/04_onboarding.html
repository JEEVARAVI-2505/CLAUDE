Part 4 — Beginner Onboarding Programme

Goal: in the first week every new employee is legally briefed, equipped with a secure workstation, assigned a mentor, and able to practise safely in the isolated lab. Technical depth comes later (Part 5). Trust comes before tools.

Why legal training comes before tools

A beginner with a scanner and no legal understanding is a risk to the client, the company and themselves. No trainee receives tool access or lab credentials until the quiz and acknowledgement are on file.

4.4 Code of Ethics (sign on Day 1)

  1. I will test only with written authorisation and only inside the agreed scope.
  2. I will protect client data and evidence as if it were my own most sensitive information.
  3. I will use my skills to defend, never to harm, and I will report discoveries only to authorised contacts.
  4. I will be honest in findings, including about what I do not know or did not test.
  5. I will stop work and report if I see danger, an unexpected impact, or a doubt about authority.
  6. I will not boast about, trade in or publish vulnerabilities or client details.
  7. I will keep learning and ask for supervision when I reach the edge of my competence.
  8. I will not discriminate against, harass or intimidate colleagues, clients or the public.
  9. I will declare conflicts of interest, including any personal link to a client or target.
  10. I accept that breaches of this code may lead to dismissal and legal action.

4.7 Mentor, supervision and probation

  • Mentor: a Consultant or Engineer at A2+ who meets the trainee weekly (30 minutes), reviews work products and signs weekly progress.
  • Observer rule: trainees shadow real engagements as silent observers only after Day 30 and only if the client has been told a trainee is present.
  • Probation reviews: Day 30, 60 and 90 with Security Lead; outcome recorded as Proceed / Proceed with support plan / Extend / Stop.
  • Escalation: concerns about workload, ethics or a colleague's behaviour go to the mentor or Security Lead; retaliation is not tolerated.
