Part 5 — 90-Day Training Roadmap

The roadmap takes a beginner through the seven training levels and eight practical projects in thirteen weeks. It assumes about 40 hours a week: roughly 35% learning, 45% hands-on lab, 10% documentation, 10% mentoring and review. Pass marks and times are suggested defaults for management approval.

Failing a gate is not a punishment: the trainee receives a support plan and re-attempts after 2 weeks. A policy breach (such as scanning a non-lab target) triggers an immediate Security Lead review regardless of scores.

5.7 After day 90: continuing professional growth

  • Months 4–6: join three supervised real engagements as A1 (Tier 0–1 tasks), write draft findings, attend client debriefs as observer.
  • Months 6–12: pursue recognised external certifications or training suited to the chosen role. Certifications are the individual's earned credentials, listed in proposals only when actually held.
  • Promotion to A2 requires at least three engagements with QA-reviewed work, a clean policy record, and Security Lead approval.
  • Map roles to the NIST NICE framework to guide development and hiring.
