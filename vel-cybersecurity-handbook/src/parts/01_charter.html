Part 1 — Department Charter and Service Portfolio

1.1 Mission and operating principles

Mission: help clients find, understand and reduce security risk through authorised, documented, defensive security work, delivered by well-trained people working under qualified supervision.

PrincipleWhat it means in daily work
Authorised onlyEvery test, scan and access has a signed authorisation and a defined scope.
Defensive purposeWe find weaknesses so they can be fixed. We do not build or deploy attack tools against third parties.
Least intrusive methodConfiguration review before scanning; scanning before manual verification; manual verification only where safe.
Supervised growthAuthority is earned through training, assessment and observed practice (the authority ladder in Part 2).
Evidence-basedFindings are confirmed, reproducible within scope, and backed by sanitised evidence.
Honest limitsReports state what was and was not tested, and what we cannot conclude.
Client data is sacredMinimise, encrypt, restrict, retain only as agreed, then securely delete.

1.3 What we will not do

  • Scan, test, log in to, or probe any system we do not have written authority to test.
  • Run credential attacks, malware deployment, denial-of-service, social engineering or physical intrusion unless management has formally added that service (with legal and insurance review) and the client has authorised that exact activity in writing (Tier 3).
  • Access, copy or keep more client data than the assessment needs.
  • Sell "guaranteed secure" or "unhackable" outcomes. No assessment proves the absence of vulnerabilities.
  • Make legal decisions, including whether a breach must be notified to a regulator.
