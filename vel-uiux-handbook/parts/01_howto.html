How to Use This Handbook

This is Part 1 of 17 in the UI/UX Department operating system. It does three jobs: (1) defines what the department is, who does what and how work is approved; (2) onboards a new employee with basic computer skills and little design experience; (3) gives a day-by-day 90-day programme that takes them to Junior UI Designer (supervised). Parts 2 to 17 teach each subject in full.

Golden rules of the department
  1. Do not skip stages. A stage starts only when the previous approval gate is signed.
  2. Design decisions come from user needs and project goals, never from personal taste alone.
  3. Never invent research. If an interview or usability test did not happen, no document may say it did.
  4. Never promise higher conversion, revenue or "WCAG compliance" without measured evidence.
  5. Never copy a competitor's design. Research informs; it does not supply the answer.
  6. Every deliverable has a named reviewer who is not its author.
  7. Figma is the source of truth for design; the repository stores documents, approved exports and evidence.
