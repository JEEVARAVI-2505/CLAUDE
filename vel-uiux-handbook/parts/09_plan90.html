8. 90-Day Training Programme

13 weeks in three phases. Every week ends with a deliverable reviewed by a named reviewer. The programme covers Levels 1 to 4 fully, and introduces Levels 5 to 9 at awareness-plus-practice depth; full mastery of those and Projects 6 to 10 continues in Days 91 to 180 (Part 17).

Phase 1 · Foundations · Days 1-30➜Phase 2 · Users and Systems · Days 31-60➜Phase 3 · Products and Practice · Days 61-90

Checkpoint C Day 90 final competency reviewPanel: UI/UX Lead, Senior Product Designer, PM. Evidence: portfolio of Projects 1 to 5, audit starter, learning log, quiz scores, mentor report. Outcome: Certified Junior (supervised), Extend 30 days with a written plan, or Not certified.
