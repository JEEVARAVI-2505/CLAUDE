2. Roles, Responsibilities and Review Authority

A startup may have one person in several roles. That is allowed, but the author of a deliverable may never be its only approver: the final-review owner below must be named on every project.

RoleCore responsibilitiesTypical outputsOwns gates
Junior UI DesignerFigma fundamentals, screen creation, component use, responsive layouts, documentationScreens, component instances, exports, notesPrepares; approves none
UX DesignerResearch, journeys, wireframes, information architecture, usability evaluationPersonas, sitemaps, flows, wireframes, test reportsPrepares G2, G3, G7
Senior Product DesignerProduct strategy, design direction, design systems, complex product interfaces, design reviewsDesign system, direction board, review notesReviews G4, G5, G6, G8
UI/UX LeadProject architecture, quality standards, client presentation, final design approvalApproved design package, acceptance recordApproves G4 to G9; presents G10
Project ManagerTimeline, client communication, milestones, resources, change managementPlan, status reports, change requests, approval logOwns G1 and G10 paperwork
