9. Assessment, Competency Levels and Sign-off

Weights and pass marks below are a proposal for management approval.

ComponentWeightHow measuredAssessor
Practical projects 1 to 550 %Rubric below, scored out of 100 per projectSenior / Lead
Knowledge assessments (5 quizzes)20 %Closed-book, 20 questions eachMentor
Professional practice15 %Naming, file hygiene, documentation, learning log, punctuality of deliveriesMentor
Communication and review response15 %Quality of design explanations, handling feedback, asking for helpLead + buddy

Pass rule: overall ≥ 70 % and each component ≥ 60 % and no breach of the golden rules (for example, invented research is an automatic fail of that deliverable).

Project scoring rubric (100 points)

CriterionPtsWhat a full score looks like
Brief fit and user focus15Solves the stated objective; every key decision has a written reason
Visual hierarchy and layout20Clear focal point; consistent grid and spacing from the scale
Typography and colour15Defined type scale; palette from tokens; all text pairs meet 4.5:1 (or 3:1 large)
Components and consistency15Reusable components, variants, no detached styles or magic numbers
States and responsiveness15Hover, focus, disabled, loading, error, empty, success; desktop, tablet, mobile
Accessibility checks10Focus visible, targets ≥ 24px (44px preferred), colour not sole signal, alt text notes
Documentation and file hygiene10Named layers/pages, design-decisions note, export-ready, correct file names
