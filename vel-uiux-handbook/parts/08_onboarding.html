Working standards from Day 1
  1. File names: YYYY-MM-DD_Project_Screen_v01. Pages named by purpose: Cover, Research, Wireframes, UI, Components, Archive.
  2. Layer names always meaningful. "Rectangle 214" is a defect.
  3. Ask for help after 30 minutes stuck. Not asking is the bigger risk.
  4. Never publish or share a Figma link with "anyone can edit".

Support and escalation

Technical or tool problem: buddy, then mentor. Unclear requirement: mentor, then PM. Quality dispute: UI/UX Lead decides. Concern about conduct or safety: management, in confidence.
