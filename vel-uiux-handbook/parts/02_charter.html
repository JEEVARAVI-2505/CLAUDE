1. Department Charter

Mission

Run a repeatable, quality-controlled Digital Product Design operation that designs websites, mobile apps, SaaS and enterprise products and AI interfaces for international clients, and trains junior employees to deliver that work to a professional standard.

This is a design engineering department, not a freelance graphic studio

Freelance graphic studioVEL UI/UX department
Starts from "make it look nice"Starts from a business objective and a defined user
Delivers images or one-off filesDelivers a design system, prototype and developer-ready handoff
Feedback is open-endedScope, revision limits and acceptance criteria are written and signed
Quality depends on the individualQuality is checked by gates, checklists and an independent reviewer
One-time jobMonthly design-support retainer after delivery

Claims we never makeWe never promise increased conversion, revenue or retention without measured evidence. We say "WCAG 2.2 level AA target, verified by testing", never "fully accessible" or "compliant" from appearance alone. We never present AI-generated content in an interface as verified fact.

Department principles

User-led

Every decision traces to a user need, a research finding or a project objective, and is written in design-decisions.md.

System-first

Tokens and components before screens. A value used twice becomes a token.

Evidence-based

Approvals, test notes and sources are stored. No evidence, no claim.

Accessible by default

Contrast, focus, target size and keyboard order are designed in, not patched later.

Original work

Learn from references; never copy them. Only licensed fonts, images and icons.

Confidential

Client files stay in approved tools. Do not duplicate client assets into the repository unnecessarily.
