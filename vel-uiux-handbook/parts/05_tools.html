Licensing rules
  1. No cracked or "free-download" software. Every seat is licensed or uses a legitimately free plan.
  2. Fonts, icons, photos and illustrations must have a licence that allows commercial client use. Record licence and source in assets/ notes.
  3. VEL's own documents use Inter (SIL Open Font License), Liberation Serif/Times and JetBrains Mono (OFL) which are free for commercial use; confirm the licence text when you download a font.
  4. Client files and credentials never go into personal accounts or public AI tools.

Installation and account set-up (all new employees)

  1. Create the company Figma account with your VEL email; accept the team invitation; do not use a personal email.
  2. Turn on two-factor authentication for Figma, GitHub, Notion and email.
  3. Install the Figma desktop app (or use the browser version) and the Inter font; check Figma's current system requirements  VERIFY .
  4. Open the "VEL Training Sandbox" team project. Create a file named YYYY-MM-DD_Firstname_Day1.
