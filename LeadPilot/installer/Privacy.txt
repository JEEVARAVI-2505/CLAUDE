LeadPilot AI - privacy and data handling
=========================================

Where your data lives
  All leads, contacts, notes, tasks and settings are stored on this computer in:
      %LOCALAPPDATA%\LeadPilotAI
  API keys you enter are encrypted with Windows DPAPI for your Windows account.

What is sent over the internet - only when you use the feature
  * Lead Finder (OpenStreetMap): the place name and business categories you search for.
  * Website reading: ordinary page requests to the websites of companies already in your list.
  * Optional web search (Brave Search, your key): company name and city.
  * Optional AI: with Ollama, nothing leaves your computer; with an OpenAI-compatible provider, the text of the prompt (which may include lead details) goes to that provider.

What is NOT sent
  No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no lead data, and nothing to the software vendor.

Your responsibilities
  Business contact details that are published online are not automatically free to use for marketing. Follow the anti-spam, telemarketing and
  data-protection laws of your country (for example consent and opt-out rules). LeadPilot AI includes an opt-out line in drafted e-mails and
  lets you export or delete all data at any time.

Uninstalling keeps your data unless you choose to delete it.
