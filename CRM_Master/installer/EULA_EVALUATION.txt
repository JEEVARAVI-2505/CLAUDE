CRM MASTER - EVALUATION NOTICE

This is an evaluation build of CRM MASTER, provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

- All processing happens on your computer. The application does not send your contact data anywhere.
- Your data is stored in your Windows user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\CRM Master). Back it up regularly (Settings > Backup and restore).
- Data-cleaning, validation, duplicate-detection and bulk-update results are produced by local rules and heuristics. Review them before
  relying on them. Email checks are syntax-only and do not prove that a mailbox exists.
