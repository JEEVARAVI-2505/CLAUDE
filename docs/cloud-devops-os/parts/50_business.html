Mandatory fields for every project record

Unique project ID · client ID · assigned employee · project manager · current stage · milestones · due dates · approval status · cost status · risk register · delivery status.

SecurityThe portal must never store cloud root passwords, API keys or private keys. Link to the approved secrets manager instead. Protect the portal with SSO/MFA and audit logs.

Required financial outputs

Direct project cost · internal labour · third-party cost · cloud cost · contingency · total cost · selling price · gross profit · gross margin · MRR · ARR · break-even · renewal price · additional usage charges.

Formulas

Gross profit      = Revenue - Direct costs
Gross margin %    = Gross profit / Revenue x 100
Contribution      = Revenue - Variable costs
Break-even (units)= Fixed costs / (Price per unit - Variable cost per unit)
MRR               = Sum of monthly recurring fees
ARR               = MRR x 12
Cost per user     = Monthly cloud cost / Active users

Assumptions: labour 120 hours at an internal cost of 20 per hour; tools 100; contingency 15% of subtotal; target profit 30% of total cost. Currency units are generic.

Client Acquisition and Deal Closing

Target customers: SaaS startups; software companies; healthcare; e-commerce; manufacturing; financial and professional services; growing SMEs; businesses on outdated infrastructure.

Channels: LinkedIn; Upwork; Clutch; direct email; technology partnerships; startup communities; referrals; existing web/app development clients.

15-step sales process

  1. Identify potential customer.
  2. Research their technology.
  3. Find a real infrastructure problem.
  4. Contact the decision-maker.
  5. Schedule discovery.
  6. Conduct technical assessment.
  7. Prepare solution proposal.
  8. Present architecture.
  9. Explain implementation and recurring costs.
  10. Offer a paid pilot where appropriate.
  11. Agree scope and contract.
  12. Obtain payment and authorisation.
  13. Start delivery.
  14. Demonstrate results.
  15. Convert to a managed-services contract.
Honesty ruleNever promise savings, performance or security outcomes without supporting evidence. Say “we will measure and report,” not “you will save 40%.”

Template: discovery questionnaire (short form)

  • What is the business problem and what does success look like?
  • Where does the application run today and who maintains it?
  • How many users, and what happens if it is down for an hour?
  • What data do you hold and are there regulatory duties?
  • When was the last backup restored successfully?
  • What is the monthly hosting bill, budget range and deadline?
  • Who approves scope and payment?
