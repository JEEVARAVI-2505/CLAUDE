## 10. Acceptance
Each deliverable is accepted when it meets its criteria. The client has `[5]` working days to accept or list defects against the criteria; silence is not acceptance unless stated here. Defects are corrected at no charge; new requirements are change requests.

## 11. Warranty and support
`[60/90]` days warranty for defects against the accepted scope from go-live. Support and maintenance after warranty are provided only under a separate **Managed Service** schedule (monthly fee, hours included, response targets, exclusions). No uptime or security guarantee is given beyond the service schedule; **we state the tests performed, not that the system is "secure"**.

## 12. Intellectual property and licences
Define ownership of deliverables on full payment; supplier retains pre-existing tools and generic components; third-party and open-source components remain under their own licences (listed in the handover pack).

## 13. Confidentiality and data protection
Both parties keep the other's information confidential. Personal data is processed only to deliver the services and protected as described in `docs/security.md`. A data-processing agreement is attached where required.

## 14. Security and access
Access is granted to named individuals with individual accounts and MFA; no shared admin credentials; access is removed at handover unless a managed plan applies.

## 15. Change control
