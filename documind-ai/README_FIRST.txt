DocuMind AI Professional  -  START HERE
=======================================

WHAT IT IS FOR
  You receive supplier invoices and purchase orders (PDF, scans, phone photos).
  DocuMind reads them, checks the maths, lets you confirm each one, and gives your
  accountant a clean Excel "purchase register". Everything stays on your computer.

HOW TO OPEN IT (no typing needed)
  1. Double-click   START_DOCUMIND.bat
     - The first time it sets itself up (a few minutes, internet needed once).
     - If Python is missing it installs it for you.
     - After that, the same file opens the program straight away.
  2. A setup guide appears. Answer the 5 short questions (you can skip them).
  3. On the Home screen click   "Try it with sample documents"   to see everything work.

CHECK THAT IT WORKS ON THIS PC
  Double-click   CHECK_THAT_IT_WORKS.bat   - it must say  SMOKE OK.

IF THE PROGRAM DOES NOT OPEN
  Double-click   START_DOCUMIND_SHOW_ERRORS.bat   and send what it shows.

THE WHOLE JOB IS THREE STEPS
  1  Add documents       drag your files into the big box
  2  Check and approve   compare with the page, fix anything wrong, press Approve
  3  Export to Excel     pick "Purchase register", choose the month, save
